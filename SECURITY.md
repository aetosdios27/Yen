# Security policy

Yen handles community funds, so we take reports seriously.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please **do not open a public issue**. Use GitHub's private reporting:
[Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/aetosdios27/Yen/security/advisories/new).

Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and the impact you expect. We will acknowledge within 3 working days and keep you updated until it is resolved.

## Scope

In scope: the code in this repository and our deployed services. Out of scope: social engineering of our team, denial-of-service by volume, and findings that need physical access.

## What we will never do

Edit or delete recorded events, bypass an approval, or hold users' funds. If you find a path that allows any of these, that is a high-severity report.

# Personal data inventory

This inventory describes the schema at migration 0020. Retention durations are operational defaults for `runRetentionJob` in `packages/db/src/retention.ts`, not statutory interpretations. This change adds consent and erasure record tables but does not implement their user-facing API flows.

| Table / columns | Purpose and basis | Default retention | Deletion behavior | Event log / chain |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `users.id`, `phone`, `display_name`, `locale`, `assurance`, `created_at`, `erased_at`, `retention_required`, `retention_reason` | Account, sign-in and user-visible label; service/account operation | Account lifetime; expired accounts have no automated purge | Mutable account table; event references use pseudonyms | NO |
| `orgs.id`, `name`, `legal_form`, `owner_id`, `created_at` | Club administration | While club exists; retention may be required for financial records | Correctable ordinary table | NO |
| `memberships.fund_id`, `user_id`, `role`, `status`, `created_at` | Access control | Membership lifetime | Remove or deactivate with account | NO |
| `pseudonyms.pseudonym_id`, `user_id`; `fund_pseudonyms.user_id`, `fund_id`, `pseudonym_id` | Link account to pseudonymous historical activity | Account lifetime | Erasable mapping; historical event actor remains | NO |
| `sessions.id`, `user_id`, `token_hash`, `user_agent`, `device_info`, `ip_address`, timestamps, `revoked_at`; `trusted_devices.session_id`, `trusted_at`, `last_active_at`, `idle_expires_at`, `locked_at` | Authentication and session security | Sessions 30 days after expiry; trusted device follows session | Revoke/delete | NO |
| `auth_otps.id`, `phone`, `otp_hash`, `salt`, attempts, channel, expiry and timestamps; `auth_otp_attempt_limits.phone`, counters and timestamps | OTP authentication and abuse limits | OTP challenges older than 24 hours | `runRetentionJob` purges challenges; `auth_otp_attempt_limits` currently has no automated purge | NO |
| `user_approval_pins.user_id`, salt/hash and timestamps | Confirm approval PIN | Account lifetime | Delete with account | NO |
| `waitlist_entries.id`, name, contact, kind, club name, locale, consent text/version, created_at | Pilot contact requests | Until withdrawal or pilot closure; review at 12 months | Delete by normalized contact | NO |
| `display_names.fund_id`, `pseudonym_id`, `display_name`; `guest_display_names.fund_id`, `contact_hash`, `display_name`; `display_name_contact_salts.fund_id`, salt | Public label choices and guest deduplication | Fund lifetime or correction request | Correct/delete mappings; contact hashes are mutable | NO |
| `owner_invites.id`, `fund_id`, `owner_id`, `phone`, `member_name`, `secret_hash`, status and timestamps | Invite and membership onboarding | 30 days after expiry | `runRetentionJob` purges expired invites | NO |
| `user_notification_preferences.user_id`, channels, quiet hours, locale, timestamps | Notification delivery choices | Account lifetime | Delete on erasure; withdrawal disables channel | NO |
| `notification_outbox.id`, fund/user IDs, event/idempotency IDs, template data, channel/status/error/cost and timestamps; `notification_dead_letter` notification/fund/user IDs, idempotency/template data, error/cost and timestamps | Deliver and diagnose notifications | 30 days after terminal status | `runRetentionJob` purges terminal outbox and dead-letter rows | NO |
| `fund_notification_daily_usage.fund_id`, date, counts and cost | Notification cost controls | 400 days | Purge older rows | NO |
| `donation_pledges` donor contact/name, UTR, fund, amount and timestamps | Record donation pledge and receipt contact | Fund financial record lifetime; keep only if retention is required | Erase contact/name otherwise | NO |
| `expenses`, `expense_approvals`, `bank_transactions`, `anchors` | Financial operations and integrity evidence; some rows may contain uploader/actor identifiers | Fund lifetime; may be retained where another law requires | Never delete event/Merkle records; erase mutable identity links where allowed | NO |
| `fund_events` actor pseudonym, type, timestamp, payload, hashes and canonical envelope | Append-only public audit history | Indefinite, append-only | Never update or delete | NO |
| `idempotency_keys.key`, request hash/response and timestamps; `rate_limits.key`, counters and expiry; `auth_otp_attempt_limits.phone`, counters and timestamps | Replay prevention and abuse controls | Idempotency 30 days; rate limits until expiry; OTP limits: no configured automated purge | `runRetentionJob` purges idempotency and expired rate-limit rows | NO |
| `erasure_requests` user, request and completion timestamps/status; `consent_records` user, purpose, notice version, grant/withdrawal timestamps; `retention_audit` user/actor/reason/time | Privacy request, consent and retention audit | 7 years as conservative operational default; review required | Retain audit evidence; no event-log writes | NO |

“Legal basis” above identifies the product purpose or operational basis, not a legal conclusion. Erasure execution and fund-owner retention marking are not implemented. The schema supports a `retention_required` marker and reason field but does not yet enforce that only fund owners set it or create an audit row automatically. India DPDP Act 2023 erasure may yield to retention required by another law (sections 12(3), 8(7)); the design assumes the stricter reading and this document is not legal advice.

No personal-data column is an input to event payload construction or Merkle leaves. Events carry fund-scoped pseudonym identifiers; display names and contact hashes live in ordinary tables. This is a design invariant, not a claim that the not-yet-implemented consent and erasure workflows are operational.

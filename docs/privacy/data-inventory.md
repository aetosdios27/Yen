# Personal data inventory

This inventory was checked against the database schema, auth and notification stores, donation and reconciliation stores, and event payload schemas. Retention periods below are application defaults, configurable through `runRetentionJob`; they are not statutory conclusions. The purpose column records engineering use only. No legal basis is determined here; that requires qualified legal review. Every row states whether data can reach `fund_events`, its Merkle leaves, or the chain: it must be **NO**.

Consent grant and withdrawal, erasure preview/execution, and owner-authorized retention marking are available through authenticated `/v1/privacy` API routes. Erasure is transactional and leaves `fund_events` and anchors untouched. A dry run reports affected and retained row counts before changes. The owner retention endpoint requires fund ownership and target membership, then writes a reason to `retention_audit`. This engineering inventory records purpose-specific consent where the API supports it; whether consent is the legally sufficient basis for each purpose has not been assessed.

The design assumes the stricter reading: keep identifiable data only where a fund owner marks it `retention_required` and gives a logged reason. India DPDP Act 2023 sections 12(3) and 8(7) allow erasure to yield to retention required by another law; this document is engineering documentation, not legal advice.
